Process

From physical to virtual, and from concept to reality

Physical Property Scan

  • I go to your property to take the initial scan. This process only takes 1 - 2 hours.
  • I then process and optimize the data, creating a lightweight browser based tour that requires no additional app.
  • Highlights are added to the tour to showcase all the best features of the property. Including any and all highlights you would like to feature.
  • Finally you are given a live hosted link, ready to implement into your website or funda posting.

Developments and Renovations

  • Is your property currently being constructed, or in the process of being remodeled? It's still possible to feature a 3d tour of your property to jumpstart renting.
  • I can create a photo realistic rendering of your property based on the floor plans. This is then processed in the same way to create a 3D tour of your upcoming property.
